## paint

Asset: assets/krishna-heads-paint.png

Use case: identity-preserve. Asset type: production head-turn animation sprite sheet, 3 by 3 equal cells on square canvas. Reference image: use ONLY the face, hair, gold crown, peacock feather and neck of Krishna from the attached atlas as identity/style reference. Generate NINE views of the SAME head, not body parts. This is a subtle smooth head turn, not different characters. Reading cells left to right then top to bottom: head yaw -20,-15,-10,-5,0,+5,+10,+15,+20 degrees; negative means nose toward viewer's LEFT, positive toward viewer's RIGHT. CENTER CELL is EXACT STRAIGHT-ON FRONT VIEW with both eyes looking directly at the viewer, symmetric face, warm serene attentive expression, closed relaxed mouth. Each cell has identical scale, fixed neck anchor horizontally centered at 88 percent cell height, crown top at 8 percent, same eye-line at 53 percent. Full crown, feather and shoulder-length hair in every cell. No shoulders or chest. Only head+shortneck. Allow ample magenta padding so NOTHING touches or crosses cell boundaries. All nine heads are upright, same facial proportions and lighting, no morphing or exaggerated angle. Face turns anatomically around neck; NOT a mirrored copy (crown/feather retain consistent side). Expressive devotional paint brushwork, midnight blue skin, azure highlights and luminous gold, match reference. Perfectly flat solid RGB255,0,255 magenta background, no gridlines, text, labels, checkerboard or shadows on background. Preserve the reference Krishna identity, crown jewels, tilak and peacock feather. The intended experience is Krishna facing the reader personally, occasionally glancing to the verse and back.
